Cooking is easier when you know exactly what to buy and what to do next.

Why this website was made

Many people want to cook a meal but do not know the ingredients or the steps. Whit Cooks was made to give you both in seconds, so you can cook with confidence.

About us

We are a small team that loves food. We share simple recipes from Kenya and around the world, written in clear and easy words.
