Why WhitCooks Was Made

Looking up a recipe online shouldn't feel like navigating an obstacle course. Too often, home cooks just want to know what ingredients to buy and how to prepare a dish—only to be met with endless scrolling, cluttered layouts, and auto-playing advertisements.

WhitCooks was created with a single purpose: to give everyday home cooks instant access to accurate ingredients and clear cooking steps across diverse world cuisines.

Who It Is For

Busy students, home cooks exploring new international flavors, and anyone who appreciates clean, structured recipes.

Our Design Standard

Every dish includes photography, prep and cook times, serving counts, and an interactive ingredient checklist.
